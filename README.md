## 📖 Overview
<a id="overview"></a>

**Cubase** is the leading Digital Audio Workstation (DAW) developed by Steinberg for Windows, designed for audio editing, mixing, and music production. With its comprehensive feature set and more than 30 years of industry experience, Cubase is trusted by professional musicians, producers, and sound engineers worldwide. 

The **latest version** of **Cubase** includes a complete functionality that enhances your creative process, enabling you to record, edit, mix, and produce music like a professional. Whether you're working on a home studio project or a large-scale production, **Cubase** has everything you need.

## ⚡ Key Features
<a id="key-features"></a>

### Core Functionality:
- **Unlimited Tracks** — Record and mix an unlimited number of audio and MIDI tracks to create complex compositions.
- **Advanced Audio Engine** — Experience pristine sound quality with a 32-bit floating-point audio engine supporting up to 192 kHz.
- **MixConsole** — A professional mixing environment that allows for detailed control over your mixes.
- **Smart Compositional Tools** — Utilize features like the Chord Track, Chord Pads, and advanced Chord Assistant for creative inspiration.
- **Extensive Effects Library** — Access nearly 100 audio effects and instruments to enhance your sound.

### Advanced Features:
- **MIDI Controller Surface Editor** — Supports trigger pads and horizontal faders for a more intuitive workflow.
- **VST Instrument Support** — Up to 64 slots for VST instruments, allowing you to expand your sound palette.
- **Multi-Track Recording** — Record audio from multiple sources seamlessly.
- **Integration with External Hardware** — Compatible with synthesizers and signal processors for hardware integration.
